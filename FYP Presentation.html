Problem Statement

Blind citizens cannot vote independently or in secret

  • Elections rely on printed ballots and in-person polling stations.
  • A voter who cannot see must rely on an assistant to mark the ballot.
  • This removes the secrecy and dignity of an independent vote.
  • Barriers begin before polling day: CNIC, registration, transport, inaccessible premises.
The gap

There is no accessible, independent and secret way for blind and visually impaired citizens to cast a vote.

Sources: STEP/IFES Pakistan inclusion studies; WCAG 2.1; accessible-voting research.

Objectives & Scope

An audio-first, independent, secret ballot

In scope
  • Operable entirely by audio output and keyboard.
  • CNIC-based identity verification.
  • Exactly one encrypted vote per voter.
  • Results aggregate-only, released after close.

Primary users: blind & visually impaired voters. Customer: Government / ECP.

Proposed Solution

Accessible web app + AI assistance

Accessible by design

WCAG 2.1 — screen-reader support, full keyboard, high-contrast & large text.

Text-to-Speech ballot

Candidate names, instructions & confirmations spoken aloud.

Voice-guided assistant

Conversational help through each step (later sprint).

Secure identity

CNIC + OTP; verified as an eligible disabled voter.

Vote secrecy

Encrypted vote; the system never stores who voted for whom.

Aggregate results

Totals per party per constituency, only after close.

Sprint 1 · 19 Jun – 30 Jul 2026

Sprint 1 scope & goal

Goal: a voter can register, be verified, log in securely, and the system confirms eligibility before the ballot.

US-01 Must
As a voter, I can register with my CNIC so the system knows I am an eligible disabled voter.
US-03 Must
As a voter, I log in with CNIC + OTP so my account stays secure.
US-04 Must
As the system, I confirm a voter is registered as disabled before allowing a vote.

Sprint 1 · Implementation

What we built

  • Custom user model keyed by CNIC (voter / admin roles).
  • Registration with mock CNIC verification & consent.
  • CNIC + OTP login — passwordless for voters; OTP expiry & single-use.
  • Eligibility gate protecting the ballot route (US-04).
  • Swappable services — CNIC verifier & OTP delivery ready for NADRA / SMS.
  • Accessible templates — labels, visible focus, skip link, large text.
